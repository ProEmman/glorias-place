Fees

Fees

Every family's needs are different, so here's how fees work at Gloria's Place.

I don't have a fixed price list because the cost depends on your family's needs, such as your child's age, the days and hours you need care, and whether you qualify for funded childcare.

Funded places may be available, so please get in touch to check if your child is eligible. This could help reduce the amount you pay.

The best thing to do is get in touch for a quick chat. Tell me your child's age and the type of childcare you need, and I can give you a quote based on your requirements.
