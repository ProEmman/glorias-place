About Gloria's Place

A little more about who I am, and what your child can expect from their time with me.

I'm Gloria, and I run Gloria's Place Childminding — a registered childminder based in Dundee, DD2. My home is a warm, safe space where children are looked after with the same care and attention I'd want for my own family, whether that's for a full day, a few hours around the school run, or the school holidays.

I'm registered with the Care Inspectorate and a member of the Scottish Childminding Association (SCMA). I'm PVG checked, first aid trained, and fully insured, so you can feel confident that your child is in safe, properly vetted hands from the moment they arrive.

I care for children aged 0–12, and that includes before and after school care as well as care during the school holidays. Whether you need a regular routine built around term time, or extra support when the schools are closed, I can put together care that fits around your family's week.

I'm open weekdays from 7:30am to 6:00pm, so there's flexibility for early starts and later pick-ups either side of the working day.

Every day with me includes home-cooked meals and healthy snacks — nothing out of a packet, just proper food to keep little ones going. Days are filled with outdoor play, local outings, and art, craft and educational activities, all designed to help children learn through play in a way that feels like fun rather than schoolwork.

Funded child places may also be available, so if that's something that would help your family, it's well worth getting in touch to check your child's eligibility.

I'd love to meet you and your child and show you around. Get in touch to arrange a visit or ask any questions you might have.
