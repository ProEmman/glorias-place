Frequently asked questions

What ages do you care for?

I care for children aged 0–12, including before and after school care and school holiday care.

Are you Care Inspectorate registered?

Yes — I'm registered with the Care Inspectorate, a member of the Scottish Childminding Association (SCMA), PVG checked, first aid trained and fully insured.

Do you offer funded places?

Yes, funded child places are available — get in touch to check current availability for your child's age group.

What are your opening hours?

I'm open weekdays from 7:30am to 6:00pm, offering full-time and part-time care.

Do you provide meals?

Yes, home-cooked meals and healthy snacks are included, alongside outdoor play, local outings, and art, craft and educational activities.
