Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain English, what happens to the information you share with Gloria's Place Childminding through this website.

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Who we are

Gloria's Place Childminding is run by Gloria, a registered childminder based in Dundee, DD2 2FE.

If you have any questions about this notice, or about any information we hold about you, please get in touch:

  • Phone: 07823 716600
  • Email: Gloriasplace25@gmail.com
  • Address: Dundee, DD2 2FE

What information this website collects

The only information collected through this website is what you choose to type into the enquiry form when you contact us. That's:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give it (this is optional)
  • Your child's age range
  • The days and hours of care you need
  • Any message you add

Why we collect it

We only use these details to reply to your enquiry — for example, to answer your question or arrange a visit. We don't use them for anything else, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Our lawful basis for using your information

We rely on your consent. When you tick the consent box on the enquiry form before sending it, you're agreeing to let us store your details so we can get back to you.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details above.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry details for [retention period to be confirmed], and then delete them.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner, at any time, and we will do so unless we need to keep them for a legal reason.

Who we share it with

Your information is never sold, and it isn't shared with any third party. It's only ever used by Gloria's Place Childminding to respond to your enquiry.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you (the right of access)
  • Ask us to correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you're unhappy with how your information has been handled

To use any of these rights, just get in touch using the details at the top of this page.

ICO registration number: [to be confirmed]

What this notice covers

This notice only covers how this website handles the details you share through the enquiry form.

It doesn't cover the information gathered and used day-to-day for children in Gloria's care, such as registration forms, health details or observations. That's covered by separate policies, available on request.
